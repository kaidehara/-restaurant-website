Our Story

Born from Passion,
Served with Heart

The Golden Fork was founded by Chef Amara Hassan and sommelier David Lim — two lifelong friends who dreamed of a place where fine dining felt like coming home.

Every dish on our menu is a love letter to local farmers, seasonal produce, and the bold flavours of our region. We believe extraordinary food should be an experience, not just a meal.
